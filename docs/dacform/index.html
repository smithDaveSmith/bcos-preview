Incorporatea DAC

Designated Activity Company. A private company limited by shares, incorporated to carry out a specific activity. Minimum of two directors; the company secretary may be one of the directors.

This concept preview does not host the application form.

The live form at bcos.ie/dacform/ collects director, secretary, member and identification details and is the firm’s own authorised destination. It is not reproduced here, because a form that collects personal data belongs on the real site and nowhere else.

Open the live form on bcos.ie

Prefer to talk it through first? Contact the firm

What the form asks for

Part 1Company name (three, in order of preference), business activity, registered office and place of business.
Part 2Director details — name, address, date of birth, occupation, nationality, EEA residency and other directorships.
Part 3Company secretary, individual or corporate, and the declaration signatory.
Part 4Members and the shares to be issued.
Part 5Client identification documents and confirmation of the order.

Tell us what your company is, and we will tell you what it owes.

No obligation. If you are not sure which of these services you need, describe the company and what is coming up — that is usually enough for us to answer.
