Incorporation

Start a company on the right footing.

Four company types are available in the Republic of Ireland. The right one depends on what the business does and what structure sits around it — and it determines your directors, your secretary and your members’ exposure from day one.

The four types

LTD

Private Company Limited by Shares

The most common company type for small and medium enterprises.

Name must end
Must end in “Limited” or “Teoranta”
Directors
Minimum of one director
Secretary
The company secretary cannot also be the sole director
Members
Minimum of one shareholder
Liability
The shareholders have limited liability

Can avail of audit exemption and file abridged financial statements if it falls below the audit thresholds.

CLG

Company Limited by Guarantee

Has no share capital. Typically used by not-for-profits, representative bodies, sporting and charitable organisations, and owner management companies.

Minimum of two directors

Minimum of one member

How it runs

What we need from you

Beacon is a regulated trust and company service provider, so client identification has to be completed before an incorporation can proceed. That is the part worth preparing early — the rest is ours.

01

Company details

Three proposed names in order of preference, the precise business activity, the registered office address and the place of business — all within the Republic of Ireland for the registered office.

02

Directors

Full name, any previous names, residential address, date of birth, occupation, nationality, EEA residency, and any other directorships held in the past five years.

03

Company secretary

An individual or a corporate body. For a single-director LTD the secretary must be someone other than that director.

04

Members and shares

The shareholders and the shares to be issued — at least one ordinary share of €1.00 unless you specify otherwise.

05

Client identification

A certified copy of a recent utility bill or bank statement, plus certified photo identification, for each director and for anyone holding 25% or more of the equity or voting capital. Certification by a practising accountant, a solicitor or a member of a national police force.

Mobile phone, car insurance and health insurance bills are not accepted as proof of address.

And immediately after

Two clocks start on the day you incorporate.

A newly incorporated company has five months from incorporation to file its beneficial ownership details. Any change to the beneficial owners must be filed within 14 days.

A company’s first annual return falls due six months after incorporation. Neither of these waits for the company to begin trading.
