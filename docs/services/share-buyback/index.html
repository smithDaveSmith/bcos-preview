Redemption and buyback of shares

A company acquiring its own shares — subject to distributable reserves and to the constitution permitting it.

Applies toCompanies buying back or redeeming shares from an existing shareholder
Triggered byOn an agreed exit or reorganisation
NoteA buyback contract must be available for inspection by the members.
AuthorityCompanies Act 2014

A company may acquire its own shares from an existing shareholder by purchase, or in the case of redeemable shares by redemption or purchase. It can only do so where it has sufficient distributable reserves — profits available for distribution, or the proceeds of a fresh issue of shares made for the purpose of the acquisition. A redemption must be authorised by the constitution.

A buyback involves executing a contract with the shareholder, and that contract must be available for inspection by the members.

We prepare the necessary documentation and make the filings at the Companies Registration Office.
