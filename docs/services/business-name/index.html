Registration of a business name

Registering the name a company or an individual actually trades under, where it differs from the registered name.

Applies toA company or individual trading under a name other than its registered name
Triggered byBefore trading under the new name
AuthorityRegistration of Business Names Act 1963

A company or an individual can apply to register a business name under which it proposes to operate. A company commonly needs this where it intends to trade under a name different from the one on its certificate of incorporation.

Registering a business name does not create a separate legal entity, and it does not give the same protection as a company name. It records that a given legal person trades under a given name.

We complete the paperwork required to register the business name.
