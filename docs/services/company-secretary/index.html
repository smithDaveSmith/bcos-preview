Provision of company secretary

Beacon acts as the named company secretary, attends board meetings, prepares the minutes and advises on company law and corporate governance.

Applies toEvery Irish company. For a single-director company, the secretary must be a different individual or a corporate body.
Triggered byFrom incorporation, and continuously thereafter
NoteA company must have a secretary at all times.
AuthorityCompanies Act 2014

Every Irish company must have a company secretary. Where a company has a sole director, that director cannot also be the secretary — the role must be filled by another individual or by a corporate body.

You can engage us to act as the named company secretary. We have the expertise required for the role: we attend meetings of the board, prepare the minutes of those meetings, and advise on all matters of company law and corporate governance as they arise.

In practice this is the service that removes the most risk, because the obligations in this register stop being something you have to remember.
