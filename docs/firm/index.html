What we do

We hold the record, so you can run the business.

We provide a full range of services that let our clients get on with the day-to-day running of their businesses — corporate compliance, technical and advisory work, for clients based in Ireland, the UK, Europe, the US and Asia.

We also provide a full company secretarial audit and review, establishing that the regulatory and procedural elements of an enterprise are both compliant with current legislation and operating to best practice. It is usually the right first step where a company has changed advisers, grown quickly, or simply gone some years without anyone looking at the register as a whole.

In all of our relationships we bring a strong commitment to excellence of service, accountability, honesty and respect for the confidentiality of our clients.

Our people

Four people. You will know which one files.

A small team is the point. There is no account manager between you and the person who prepares the minutes and makes the filing.

Principal
